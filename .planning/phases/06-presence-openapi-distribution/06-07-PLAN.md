| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-07-01 | Information disclosure | /admin/presence exposes player Guids + (optionally) display names to admins | accept | The panel is gated by AdminPolicies.Admin (Phase 3 — admins are the operator); exposing the player list to operators is the explicit feature (PRES-06). | T-06-07-02 | EoP | Non-admin player attempts to navigate to /admin/presence | mitigate | `@attribute [Authorize(Policy = AdminPolicies.Admin)]` on the page enforces the policy; the Phase 3 cookie auth handler returns 404 (Production) or 302 to /admin/login (Development) per Phase 3 D-02 cookie-events matrix. |
| T-06-07-03 | DoS | Multiple admin tabs polling every 10s could hammer Redis | accept | Each tab is one SCAN-based KeysAsync call per 10s — bounded to 25 results; cost is sub-millisecond against a typical Redis with thousands of keys. Concurrent-admin scaling identical to Health panel. |
