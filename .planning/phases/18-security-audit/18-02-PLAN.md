<objective>
Fix the real GDPR right-to-erasure bug: `DeletePlayerAsync` currently deletes only the `players` row and relies on DB cascades, but `party_members.PlayerId` (Matchmaking) and `account_merges.TargetPlayerId` (Auth) use `OnDelete(Restrict)`, so deleting a player who is a non-owner party member OR the surviving target of a merge throws Postgres 23503 and rolls back the entire erasure. Fix it package-boundary-clean via a new Core `IGdprDeleteExtension` interface (Option A from RESEARCH §SEC-04) that Auth and Matchmaking implement, then prove completeness with an all-FK-tables integration test.

Purpose: A player exercising their GDPR right-to-erasure must be fully deleted regardless of party membership or merge history — currently they cannot be.
Output: `IGdprDeleteExtension` (Core), two implementations (Auth, Matchmaking), updated `GdprDeleteService`, and `GdprDeleteCoverageTests` that FAILS before the fix and PASSES after.
</objective>
